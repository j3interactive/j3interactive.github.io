About

The studio

J3 Interactive is a small independent studio that makes games we'd want to play ourselves.

We focus on tight controls, bold visuals and short sessions that keep you coming back for one more run. Every game starts as a quick prototype and only gets built out if it's genuinely fun.

We're always happy to hear from players, publishers, press and anyone who wants to collaborate.
